01 — Why Join Lakeside

Everything You Need To Feel Good

We’re built around people, not just equipment. Here is what you will find when you walk through our doors.

Upgraded Gym Suite

A fully-kitted floor with free weights, squat racks, cardio machines, and functional training areas.

Racquet Sports & Hall

Dedicated courts for Squash, Handball, and Racquetball, plus a full indoor hall for team sports.

Sauna & Recovery

On-site sauna suite to help you recover faster and feel refreshed post-workout.

Group Classes

Spinning, strength, and mobility classes coached by instructors focused on your progress.

Coaching & Guidance

Friendly coaches available to help you build confidence, set goals, and get results safely.

Ballinrobe Community

The core of our club. A non-intimidating, supportive culture where members belong from day one.
